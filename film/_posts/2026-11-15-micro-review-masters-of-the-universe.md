Despite being made by a different production company and principal crew, and not being as well-received, I consider this movie a spiritual follow-up to *Barbie*. In a similar fashion to the 2023 film, *Masters of the Universe* turns a familiar Mattel doll brand into a reflection of contemporaneous gender expectations. They turned a decidedly macho character what many (in the West) would consider a male role model for kids to look up to. This iteration of He-Man not only is buff and brave, but also has heart and leads with dialogue. This movie is woke in all the best ways. Young boys should be encouraged to watch it. It's got gorgeous visuals and a background score that does much heavy lifting. I almost recommend it just for the music. I just wish they committed more to the goofiness that's present in small doses.

**8/10 ★★★★★★★★☆☆**
